Delete your WowCity account

You can delete your account yourself in the app at any time, or ask us to do it for you.

WowCity shopper app (com.luzzan.wowcity)

  1. Open the WowCity app and sign in.
  2. Go to Account.
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm.

What is deleted: your login (email or mobile number), your name and all saved favourites, straight away.
What is kept: nothing linked to you. Security logs without your account are kept for a short time and then removed.

WowCity Seller app (com.luzzan.wowcity.seller)

  1. Sign in as the shop owner (staff cannot delete the shop).
  2. Go to Settings → Delete account.
  3. Type your shop code to confirm and tap Delete account.

What is deleted: the owner's login, and the shop's listing in the shopper app. Every staff member and device is signed out.
What is kept: bills, tax records and ledgers, only for as long as Indian tax law requires, and then deleted. They are never shown to anyone.

Can't open the app?

Email {{site.contact.email}} from the email address on your account, or include the mobile number you sign in with. Tell us which app you use, and for a shop, the shop name or shop code. We confirm it's you, delete the account within 30 days, and email you when it's done.
