Installing the Android app

Installed in under a minute.

  1. 1
    Tap "Download for Android"

    on your phone. The file is called WowCity-Seller.apk.

  2. 2
    Open the downloaded file

    If Android asks, allow installing apps from your browser.

  3. 3
    Tap Install, then Open

    Sign in with your shop's email or mobile, using a one-time code.
